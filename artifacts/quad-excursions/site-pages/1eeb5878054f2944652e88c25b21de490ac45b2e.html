PERSONAL DATA SUBJECT TO PROCESSING

By “Personal data” it is meant any information relating to an identified or identifiable natural person (the data subject) with particular reference to an identifier such as name, identification number, location data, an online identifier or one or more elements specific to his physical, physiological, mental, economic, cultural or social identity.
The Personal Data collected on this site are:

Browsing data

The Site's IT systems collect some of your personal data whose transmission is implicit in the use of Internet communication protocols. These are information not collected to be associated with you as a data subject but which by their nature could however allow identification through processing and association with data held by third parties. These include IP addresses or domain names of devices used to connect to the Site, URI notation addresses of requested resources, time of request, method used to submit the request to the server, size of file obtained in response, numeric code indicating status of server response (success, error etc.) and other parameters related to operating system and IT environment.
These data are used in order to obtain anonymous statistical information on Site usage and to check correct functioning; to allow – given the architecture of systems used – correct delivery of various requested functionalities, for security reasons and verification of responsibility in case of hypothetical cyber crimes against the Site or third parties.

Voluntarily provided data

Through the Site you may voluntarily provide us with personal data such as name and email address via forms present on the site. Quad Excursions will process these your data respecting Applicable Legislation considering them related to you as a data subject who has provided them or expressly authorized third parties to provide them on his/her behalf based on a suitable legal basis legitimizing such processing. In such cases you as data subject and any third party providing them on your/his behalf assume all legal obligations and responsibilities releasing Quad Excursions from any disputes, claims or damage compensation requests arising from processing that may come from others whose personal data have been processed through use of the Site in violation of applicable legislation.

Cookies and similar technologies

Quad Excursions collects your personal data through cookies. Cookies are small data files that can be sent from a website to user terminal (usually browser), where they are stored then retransmitted at next visit. A cookie cannot recall any other data from user's hard drive nor transmit viruses or acquire email addresses. Cookies can be used both by site owner and third parties and can be easily disabled by user. Quad Excursions needs use some cookies including third-party ones through this Site regularly providing its Services. Therefore if you as a data subject decide disabling cookie use for purely technical reasons Quad Excursions might be unable to provide certain Services or provide them under requested conditions. More information about use of cookies and similar technologies is available in dedicated section reachable from site's footer.
