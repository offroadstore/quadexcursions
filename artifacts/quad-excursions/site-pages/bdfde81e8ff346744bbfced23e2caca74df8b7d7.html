ATV excursion from 4H | Cefalù / Castelbuono

  • Route: 50 km
  • Duration: approximately 4 hours
  • Difficulty level: Medium
  • Vehicles: Suzuki Kingquad 450

The experience is suitable for everyone, during the excursion you will be accompanied by the Quad Excursion Team

180,00€
Book now!

Ready for an adventure?

Explore Cefalù on four wheels! Wild emotions and uncontaminated nature await you in a unique quad adventure. Book your excursion now and live an unforgettable experience! 

WHAT'S INCLUDED IN THE EXCURSION?
  • Helmet
  • Single-use TNT hygienic under-helmet
  • Third-party liability insurance
  • Guide who will accompany you throughout the route
  • Petrol
  • In case of rain, rain pants and jacket
  • Short briefing on the use, safety, and basics of the Quad with practical test.
  • 1 escort or 2 depending on the number of participants (leader and ender)
  • Assistance during the route
      RECOMMENDED CLOTHING

      Comfortable clothing, closed-toe shoes, and sunglasses. Gloves are not necessary in summer, while in winter we recommend them for the cold.

      CANCELLATION

      The customer is required to communicate the cancellation of the reservation within a period of 72 hours via email. Beyond this deadline, the cost of the excursion will not be refunded.

      REQUIREMENTS

      A valid driver's license (B category), not physically altered, photographed, or photocopied.

      READY TO GO?

      The day before each excursion, you will receive a message from our Staff containing the location of the meeting point.

      Please arrive at the meeting point 15 minutes before the departure time in order to find parking nearby and complete the verification or filling out of the contract.
