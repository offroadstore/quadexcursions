Cefalù Castelbuono excursions + Lunch

  • Duration: approximately 6 hours

DESCRIZIONE

When the journey becomes an unforgettable culinary experience, every bite turns into a tale of Sicilian traditions!

The quad excursion from Cefalù to Castelbuono is a must-do experience for adventure enthusiasts, enhanced with a memorable culinary journey where each flavor narrates Sicilian traditions.

Immerse yourself fully in an exhilarating adventure, exploring unique paths and feeling the adrenaline rush of driving through varied terrains. With our experienced guides by your side, you'll depart from the splendid Cefalù to venture towards the evocative Sanctuary of Gibilmanna, traversing 14 km of asphalted roads that will lead you to discover ancient traditions and charming landscapes.

Continue the journey through the majestic mountain landscapes of Madonie, navigating unpaved trails and immersing yourself in the wild beauty of nature. Encounter the local fauna and be captivated by the magic of Sicilian territory.

Make a stop at the Bergi farmhouse before reaching Castelbuono, where you can indulge in a delicious lunch consisting of two main courses, a second course, side dishes, and dessert, prepared with typical local seasonal products!

Proceed to Castelbuono, a medieval gem nestled among the mountains, savor the exquisite local products offered by the renowned Bar Fiasconaro, and admire the picturesque SS. Maria Assunta church in the square, near the Ventimiglia Castle, historical witnesses of this millenary place. Before reaching the village, don't forget to stop to take unforgettable photos on the giant bench, perfect for preserving precious memories.

Finally, explore the lush heart of the Madonie Park, a natural paradise with forests, rivers, and breathtaking views stretching to the beautiful Aeolian Islands. An authentic experience that will provide you with unique and unforgettable emotions!
