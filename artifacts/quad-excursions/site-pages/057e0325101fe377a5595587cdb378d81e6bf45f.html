Quad excursion: offer x2 + overnight stay in Cefalù.

Sun 12 Dec 2021

Herman Geraci

Go back

Uncategorized

Cefalù is one of the most touristy and popular towns in Sicily, especially in summer. The charming views of the village overlooking the sea, the medieval historic center, and the characteristic narrow streets make Cefalù a lovely gem of the province of Palermo facing the Mediterranean.

Those who choose to visit Cefalù do so to enjoy the crystal-clear sea and the nightlife, but it has recently become a favorite tourist destination also for nature lovers and hikers.

Belonging to the Madonie Regional Natural Park, the mountainous territory surrounding and overlooking Cefalù is very suitable for exploring on foot, by bike, and by ATV. Indeed, many tourists wait for the cooler seasons to book a holiday in Cefalù and venture into natural landscapes aboard an ATV. In autumn and winter months, you can experience a Nature hike embellished by the chromatic beauty of the Madonie foliage and the candor of the first snowfalls.

This is how QuadExursions has created a special offer with CefaluApartments, a Cefaludese booking for a stay in Cefalù:

STAY X2 RESIDENCE CALAGRANDE + QUAD EXCURSION = 199€

With every Saturday overnight reservation at Residence Calagrande, you can participate in an approximately 6-hour quad excursion with organized guide. The excursion starts on Sunday morning, with lunch at a typical farm in the Madonie area (cost not included in the offer - set menu +35€). The offer is valid until 31/04/2024.

 

Quad excursions with QuadExcursions

QuadExcursions is the only one in the Cefalu area to offer excursions in the Madonie mountains to quad enthusiasts. The offer includes different experiences, suitable for both an audience that has already driven a quad and for those who are curious and eager to experience an original and adrenaline-filled adventure, crossing off-road paths through the woods and mountainous hinterland.

The quad is a very comfortable and robust vehicle, homologated for two people, 450 cc, 4-stroke 4x4, with automatic transmission for easy use.

The only condition is: having fun safely. The quad excursion package can be booked by adults who have a B driving license, and the team will provide participants:

  • a small briefing to provide information on the use, safety, and basics of the quad, along with a practical test;
  • a quad for one person plus a passenger who is at least 10 years old;
  • one or two experienced escorts, acting as lead and tail depending on the number of participants;
  • assistance during the route;
  • third party liability insurance;
  • fuel;
  • helmet with visor.

The experience is within reach of all those who intend to take this particular trip precisely because of the favorable characteristics of the excursion. The pre-departure meeting, in fact, is designed to accompany and train tourists in the safe use of the quad and in understanding the type of terrain on which they will ride.

But, of course, adventurous participants are not left alone: there will always be an experienced quad guide leading the way throughout the entire route, pointing out the necessary stops at the most scenic viewpoints, will provide information on the biodiversity of local flora and fauna, and will assist the group in any situation.

Furthermore, the two quad passengers will be provided with a helmet with visor, necessary to comply with off-road driving safety regulations. Participants in the excursion are advised to wear suitable clothing: gloves, hat, hiking shoes, long pants, heavy jacket and raincoat in winter; sneakers, spare t-shirts, sunglasses, visor cap and plenty of water in summer.

To make the quad tour more rich and enticing, QuadExcursions collaborates with CefaluApartments: the two tourist entities have joined forces to create an offer that cannot go unnoticed. The proposal includes the possibility of booking the quad excursion together with a one-night stay for two people in the residence. CalaGrande of Cefalù.
An original idea to give as a Christmas gift, for a romantic weekend getaway or for the next Valentine's Day, for a couple who loves sports and nature.
