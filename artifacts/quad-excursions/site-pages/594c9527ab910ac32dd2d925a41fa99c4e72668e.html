Our story

The idea of starting my business in the sector I love the most, enduro, was born when I decided to introduce my brother to "My World" during a Sunday in the magnificent Madonie Park, a place that many Sicilians do not know due to their sedentary lifestyle and routine.

From the first ride on a motocross bike, I was fascinated by those breathtaking landscapes, far from the usual views of dry grass along the roads.

The presence of towering trees, rivers, deer, horses, wild boars, and cows roaming freely made the experience almost surreal, as if I had stepped into a movie.

Going back to my brother, I had him rent a friend's motorcycle and we explored together the beauties of the surroundings of the Madonie Park.

He too was amazed: despite living in Sicily for 40 years, he had never noticed the beauty of his native land. Joking at the end of the excursion, wet from the rain that had surprised us during the return, we looked at each other and said laughing - "Why not turn excursions in the Madonie Park into a commercial activity?"

AND HERE WE ARE!

From enduro, I wanted to make the experience accessible to everyone, offering my clients an equally fun and safe means of transport: the Quad.

Our Quads are approved for two people, robust, comfortable and equipped with 4-wheel drive - 400 cc 4-stroke engine, with a sequential button gearbox (very easy to use) and a front winch, complying with all pollution regulations. Currently we are the only ones offering this type of service, giving our customers the opportunity to explore places that would otherwise be inaccessible without our vehicles.
