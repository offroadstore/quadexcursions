The quad excursion that can be useful for your corporate team.

Tue 08 Feb 2022

Herman Geraci

Go back

Uncategorized

Team building is an increasingly widespread practice in Italy: it brings together many companies interested in a modern and effective personnel management. Not only that. It is, in fact, a real philosophy, customizable for every professional sector and work environment that resorts to training experiences through which a group of people can achieve common goals by collaborating and... why not, having fun. Group games, recreational activities to do together, sports, etc. are some of the proposals that companies address to their human resources.

Team building is an experiential group method that stimulates and strengthens interpersonal relationships, improves organizational processes, speeds up timing in case of need for quick reactions and facilitates problem solving creatively. All with a strong collaborative spirit. This encourages the propensity to Teamwork to achieve the best result together. A result that is not just reaching a goal, but the well-being of the people involved, of the work group itself.

Benefits of team building

The benefits of team building are remarkable and specific to each group interested in such activity. It is a process of planning and sharing motivational experiences, only partially programmable, to be lived outside the office walls. It creates closer bonds among colleagues from a human point of view, bringing out difficulties and problems whose resolution is easier and more immediate in an environment different from the professional one. In this way, trust, respect, and harmony are developed.

Motivated individuals connected by feelings of mutual esteem and friendship work better together, supporting each other in achieving their own goals and those of the company. The effects of good team building are evident in a company, even if small, as it increases productivity. It is recommended for bonding a new group of people or when the team seems a bit "dull.

An investment for the company's growth

Not all entrepreneurs are already so farsighted as to understand how useful a team building activity can be. It is an investment that can allow the company significant savings and economic advantage. How? Any business searches for and trains its own staff, investing time and energy, as well as financial resources. A work environment with frequent turnover damages the company, undermines productivity, and results in a less smooth workflow. This translates into costs. Conversely, by investing in staff bonding policies, the company benefits in terms of well-being in the workplace and, consequently, Employee loyalty to the company. This increases the sense of belonging to the corporate community. Team building should therefore be accounted for as collective welfare!

Which team building activities to propose: quad excursions

Every company, in collaboration with its staff, can select the type of activities to be carried out together. One of the most appreciated practices that sensitizes the team, strengthens support for weaker colleagues, and cements relationships and collaboration involves quad excursions.

Adventure, adrenaline, outdoor activities, creativity, self-awareness… a quad excursion can be all this. One of its main advantages is given by the search for stability as a pair, since a quad is designed to face challenging routes to stay balanced on any type of road. It accommodates two people, driver and passenger. The layout is useful for creating trust even in micro teams. To drive a quad you only need a B license.

Quad biking, driving it for a team experience

Driving the quad is easy and immediate, so it does not require much practice, which is why it is suitable for anyone who wants to experience and try a different emotion. A day on the quad with colleagues is aimed at fun and relaxation, getting rid of the formalities that an office often requires and letting go of one's instincts. Jumps in the saddle along rough paths, possible falls in the mud, laughter, and collaboration are guaranteed and create relationships on a completely new level. The quality of human relationships comes first.

An quad excursion requires concentration skills, adaptability, agility (not only physical) in open environments where improvisation can and must be done. Ideal for determining and encouraging problem-solving skills, as well as reducing communication barriers. Doing something unusual, something you are not used to or that you may have never tried to do alone can represent a "break" from routine. This break can push people to adopt different behaviors, out of the ordinary, that help them think and react away from their own Comfort zone. Interrupting the routine, in collaboration with colleagues, can create new friendships or strengthen existing ones, but also unlock any moments of company deadlock, in times of operational or creative crisis or important management changes.

Quad excursions in Sicily to strengthen the bond with the company

Why not indulge in a team building experience immersed in the uncontaminated nature of Sicily? A unique opportunity to test yourself. An opportunity for individual and group growth and also an opportunity to appreciate one of Italy's most enchanting places. There is nothing better to bond a work team than a "wild" adventure to experience open air in Sicily and in complete safety.

Our proposal for quad bike excursions in Cefalù is designed to introduce you to a territory that is still largely unexplored by standard tourist routes, the Madonie Mountains, but with the conveniences of the city of Cefalù, a tourist gem in the Mediterranean. The excursions starting from Cefalù with Quad Experience are designed for teams of up to 16 people, with a guide, and lead to altitudes perfect for discovering the woods and sea views of Cefalù and the Aeolian Islands in every season.

Each excursion has a different duration, depending on the number of participants and the area to be visited. After the activity preparation phase, where safety procedures and the route to be covered are presented, people are divided into small groups that work together as part of the team.

The entire company management can take part, from executives to new employees, to strengthen relationships and not perceive leadership as a rigid vertical hierarchy. Everyone works with each other experiencing an equal status. There is no boss, but a leader who enhances the group itself and draws inspiration from it. This does not mean that tensions or conflicts cannot arise among participants within a team building project, but rather providing the means and the most suitable environment to address and overcome them. Together.
